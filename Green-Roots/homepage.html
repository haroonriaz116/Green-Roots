🔄 How it works

Three steps. Zero excuses.

01
🗂️

Sort at the source

Color-coded bins in every classroom, hallway, and the cafeteria make it obvious where each item belongs.

02
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Eco-monitors collect

Trained student volunteers do daily pickups, weigh the haul, and log it to the campus dashboard.

03
📊

Track and celebrate

We publish monthly stats and crown a winning grade with the Green Roots trophy each quarter.

🗑️ What goes where

The bin guide every locker should have.

When in doubt, ask an eco-monitor — but these three bins handle about 95% of what you'll have on a normal school day.

📘 Blue · Paper
✓ Yes
  • Notebook paper
  • Cardboard
  • Magazines
✗ Never
  • Napkins
  • Tissues
  • Wet paper
🍃 Green · Bottles & Cans
✓ Yes
  • Plastic #1 & #2
  • Aluminum cans
  • Glass bottles
✗ Never
  • Plastic bags
  • Styrofoam
  • Food containers
🟤 Brown · Compost
✓ Yes
  • Food scraps
  • Napkins
  • Lunch trays
✗ Never
  • Plastic utensils
  • Wrappers
  • Foil
