💡 Did you know?
📰
Recycling one ton of paper saves 17 trees, 7,000 gallons of water, and enough energy to power a home for 6 months.
🥤
Aluminum cans can be recycled and back on the shelf in as little as 60 days — and recycling them uses 95% less energy than making new ones.
🍂
Composting food scraps reduces methane emissions from landfills — methane is 25× more potent than CO₂ as a greenhouse gas.
🏫
Schools generate about 4 lbs of waste per student per day. If even 30% is recycled, Lincoln High could divert 1,000+ lbs a week.
🌊
Plastic that isn't recycled can take up to 450 years to break down in a landfill — and risks ending up in waterways and oceans.
🌍
If the world recycled all its aluminum, we could save enough energy to run a country the size of Italy for an entire year.
